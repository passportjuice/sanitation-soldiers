Privacy

Privacy policy.

This policy explains what Sanitation Soldiers does with the information you send us, and why.

Last updated: October 2026.

Who we are

Sanitation Soldiers is an environmental sanitation taskforce created by the Office of the Adabraka Atukpai Stool Authority. It works in Adabraka and across the Korle Klottey Constituency in Accra, Ghana. In this policy, "we", "us" and "the taskforce" mean Sanitation Soldiers and the Stool Authority office that runs it.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us. Through the contact form that is:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your phone or WhatsApp number, if you add one.
  • The location and description of the sanitation problem you report.
  • The category of the problem and any other detail you put in the message.

We do not use tracking cookies or advertising trackers on this site. We do not build a profile of you.

Why we collect it

We collect this information to read your report, find the site, act on the problem, and reply to you. Without a location and a way to reply, we cannot handle a report.

How we use it

  • To verify and act on the sanitation problem you report.
  • To contact you about the report.
  • To keep a record of reports and actions, held by the Stool Authority office.
  • To pass a matter to the Korle Klottey Municipal Assembly or another office, where the fix belongs to them.

Who we share it with

The contact form on this site is delivered by Web3Forms, a form service that carries your message to our email inbox. Web3Forms processes the form data on our behalf. Its own privacy policy applies to that processing.

The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs as part of hosting. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Where a report concerns a matter in the Assembly's hands, we may share the relevant details with the Korle Klottey Municipal Assembly so it can act.

How long we keep it

We keep report records for as long as needed to handle the report and to keep an account of the taskforce's work. You can ask us to remove your details sooner, unless we have to keep them for a legal reason.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. You can also ask us to stop contacting you. Contact the Stool Authority office using the details below to make a request.

Security

This is a static website. It has no database and no user accounts. Your report travels from the form, through Web3Forms, to a monitored inbox held by the taskforce. We take care to keep access to that inbox limited to the people who handle reports.

Children

This site is meant for residents, businesses and institutions. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If a child has sent us a report, a parent or guardian can ask us to remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and move the date at the top.
