Our authority

Founded by the Stool. Answerable to the community.

The Adabraka Atukpai Mantse and the Office of the Adabraka Atukpai Stool Authority set up Sanitation Soldiers to answer a complaint residents had raised for years: drains that choke every rainy season, refuse left where children play, and a community that had stopped expecting action.

The Stool holds the trust of Adabraka. It used that trust to put a standing team on the ground, not a one-off clean-up. The taskforce works in support of the Korle Klottey Municipal Assembly, which sets the sanitation by-laws and runs waste collection across the municipality.

Where the Assembly makes the rules, we bring the daily presence: patrols, clearing, education and a place to send what you see.

How it works

A report moves in four steps

Every report follows the same path, so you know what happens after you send it.

Where we work

  • North Adabraka
  • Official Town

  • Ringway Estates
  • Kwame Nkrumah Circle

Outside these areas, we still take reports and pass them to the right assembly or office.

Why drains come first

Adabraka sits in a low, built-up part of Accra. When drains block, water has nowhere to go, and the flooding that follows takes property and spreads disease.

Plastic, silt and household refuse block those drains faster than any crew can clear them. So we clear, and we work on the habit that stops the blocking in the first place.
