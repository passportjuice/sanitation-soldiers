What we do

Program 01

Household and commercial sanitation

Most sanitation problems in Adabraka start at the doorstep and the stall. Waste stored badly, left too long or put in the wrong place ends up in the drain or on the street.

We work with households, landlords, shopkeepers and market traders on the basics: where waste is kept, how it is separated, and when it goes to the collector.

How to reach this program

Report a compound, shop or market point where waste is piling up or being handled badly.

What it covers

  • Advice on bins, covers and keeping waste away from drains and food.
  • Checks on shared compounds and multi-household buildings.
  • Support for shops, stalls and traders on waste handling.
  • Help finding the right collection point and schedule.

Program 02

Drain desilting and clearing

Drains choke on silt, sand, plastic and household refuse. Once one blocks, a single heavy rain is enough to flood a street and send water into homes and shops.

Clearing drains is the most direct thing the taskforce does. We work the open drains, covered drains and culverts across our coverage areas, and we clear before the rains, not after them.

How to reach this program

Report a drain that is choked, covered over or giving off a bad smell. Add the nearest landmark.

What it covers

  • Clearing open drains, covered drains and culverts.
  • Removing silt, sand, plastic and refuse.
  • Reporting damaged, covered or tampered drains to the Assembly.
  • Watching the spots that block again and again.
Report a choked drain
Program 03

Public facility oversight

Public toilets, waste collection points and market sanitation serve people who have no private alternative. When they fail, the problem lands on the nearest open space or drain.

The taskforce keeps an eye on these facilities and records what it finds. Where one is closed, full or unsafe, we report it to the Assembly and the operator, then follow it up.

How to reach this program

Report a public toilet or waste point that is closed, overflowing or unsafe to use.

What it covers

  • Monitoring public toilets for access and condition.
  • Checking public waste points and containers.
  • Watching market sanitation and collection.
  • Reporting failures and tracking the response.
Report a facility problem
Program 04

Enforcement and support

By-laws only work when someone is on the ground to see them kept. The Assembly makes the rules and holds the power to enforce them. The taskforce supports that work with daily eyes and an accurate record.

Where the law is broken in a way we can document, we do so and pass it on. Where the Assembly directs community service for a minor offence, we help co-ordinate the effort.

How to reach this program

Report repeat dumping or a structure built over a drain or channel.

What it covers

  • Documenting offences such as dumping in drains.
  • Recording structures built over watercourses.
  • Supporting the Assembly's sanitation patrols.
  • Helping co-ordinate community service where the Assembly directs it.

Enforcement and sanctions rest with the Korle Klottey Municipal Assembly. The taskforce does not fine or punish.

Program 05

Community education and mobilisation

Clearing a drain fixes a drain. Teaching a street to stop filling it fixes the problem. The taskforce treats education as part of the work, not something added on at the end.

We go to households, markets, schools and community meetings with one message made local: clean drains and clean streets are a shared duty, and there is a right place for every kind of waste.

How to reach this program

Invite the taskforce to a clean-up, a school programme or a community meeting.

What it covers

  • Door-to-door and market education on waste and hygiene.
  • School visits and programmes for young residents.
  • Community clean-up days with residents and traders.
  • Working with assembly members and community groups.

How a report moves

From your message to a closed report

Every report follows the same four steps, so you know what happens after you send it.

01

Receive

A resident sends a report with a location and, where possible, a photo.

02

Verify

A team visits the site, confirms the problem and records it.

03

Act

We clear the site, escalate to the Assembly, or refer it to the office that owns the fix.

04

Close

We tell the reporter what happened and keep watch on the spot.
